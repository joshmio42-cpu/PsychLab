WHAT WAS COMPLETED
1. Secure connection: loads your Windows-encrypted Adaption credential outside
   the project. Authentication verified with a read-only official API request.
2. Fictional development data: 25 newly authored examples from all eight patient
   profiles, including personality/mental-premise context and canonical facts.
   Existing evaluation questions were held out from dataset preparation.
3. Provider run: source uploaded, 25 rows verified, quote obtained before launch,
   one adaptation job started, completion verified and output downloaded.
4. Cost controls: quote was 1 Adaption credit and 11 minutes. One 25-row run;
   no expansion, web grounding, recurring job, or automatic retries. Credits
   are not dollars; final Adaption billing is not reported by these endpoints.
5. Independent source review: 21 approved, four rejected. Two changed pronouns,
   one made a preferred approach into an asserted routine, and one returned
   invalid JSON. Exact provider output is preserved, including rejected rows.
6. Runtime connection: reviewed examples can guide the existing writer/auditor,
   with current personality and mental-premise context retained. At most two
   examples / 1,600 bytes, removed before patient facts when space is tight.
   No additional model calls. Example facts cannot be current patient citations.
7. Before/after testing: actual Featherless responses tested on the same 36
   frozen cases; an additional eight cases checked for inappropriate transfer
   of example facts, life-stage/perspective errors and unsupported details.
8. Activation decision: gates evaluated against exact saved evidence and
   independent reviews. Gate failed; the candidate remains inactive as planned.

before examples were supplied. They do not demonstrate that examples caused
the failures, but they remain operational failures with unreported token usage.

three of these cases reached example guidance, so this is limited evidence.

These are single stochastic samples with independent assistant review, not
clinical expert validation or statistical proof of improvement. Both baseline
and candidate failures remain in the evidence; no passing-only rerun was used.
