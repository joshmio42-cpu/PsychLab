Safety & privacy

Prototype notice · Updated 10 October 2026

For fictional interview practice

PsychLab is an experimental educational simulation, not a healthcare service. It does not provide therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a clinician–patient relationship. Do not use its output to make decisions about a real person's health, care, eligibility, or competence. It does not replace supervised clinical training or professional judgment.

AI and simulation limits

Patients and their histories are fictional. A case does not represent everyone with a condition. AI replies can invent details, be inconsistent, reflect bias, or contain distressing material. A frozen history does not guarantee accurate replies. Checklists, suggested questions, and debriefs are learning aids, not validated diagnostic tests or competency assessments. Verify claims against reliable sources and qualified supervision.

Not an emergency service

No clinician monitors these conversations. Do not use PsychLab for urgent help. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or seek urgent in-person care. You can stop an exercise at any time.

What happens to your information

  • Use fictional information only, including in questions, interview notes, and debriefs. Do not paste identifiable patient records, your own medical history, passwords, or other private information.
  • To generate replies, the app sends your question, relevant conversation context, and simulated case information to Featherless AI. Its published API policy says prompts and replies are not logged; that is the provider's statement, not a guarantee from PsychLab.
  • Active cases, conversations, and notes are held in the app server's memory. Sessions expire after two hours of inactivity and are removed when the app checks expired sessions; a server restart also clears that memory.
  • Answered conversations are automatically saved in this browser's local storage. Saved copies remain until deleted through History or by clearing this site's browser data. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to read them. Downloaded files must be deleted separately.
  • “Clear session and start again” removes the active server session; it does not delete saved browser history or downloaded files. Deleting History does not clear the active server session.
  • Session or visitor cookies support access and session continuity. When accessing the public domain, Cloudflare handles connection and security information, and email verification where enabled. Its processing is governed by its privacy policy. This notice does not promise anonymity or clinical confidentiality.

Access and appropriate use

Demo access is free to try but limited by AI costs and availability. The service may be interrupted, changed, or unavailable, and saved work is not guaranteed. Do not upload others' confidential or copyrighted records, attempt to bypass access or usage limits, or present simulated outputs as real patient evidence.

PsychLab is an independent project. References to diagnostic classifications, assessment tools, and AI services do not imply endorsement or clinical validation. Original reference materials and applicable permissions still matter.

For concerns about the demo or accidental disclosure, contact the project owner through the channel where you received access. Avoid including sensitive records in a report. This notice does not waive rights that apply under law.
