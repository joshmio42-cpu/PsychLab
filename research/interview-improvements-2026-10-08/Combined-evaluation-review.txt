Source and language limits

Returning or selecting a fact ID does not prove that every sentence was supported.
Facts selected by the router can be removed before generation; only actually
permitted and cited evidence can justify a reply. A cited source also does not
mean that every detail inside it was disclosed. The memory ledger tracks sources
and topics, not a certified claim-by-claim reconstruction of the transcript.

Natural shorthand still requires judgment. In the long interview, always ready
to go and easy to grab and go were loose descriptions of a stored pencil and
compact sketchbook setup. They did not supply medical findings, but concrete
wording is stronger. Clinical qualifiers need stricter handling: a frequency for
low mood cannot automatically apply to sleep or energy. Counts of paused tasks
cannot establish distress, incapacity, necessity, or cause. Ordinary interests
cannot establish current enjoyment. The earlier failures demonstrate why a green
model auditor alone is not proof of correctness.

The deterministic checks cover bounded classes of observed mistakes; they are
not a general entailment checker. Semantic auditing is fallible and may reject
a valid answer or accept an embellished one. The evaluations use fictional
authored cases and do not establish clinical validity, diagnosis accuracy,
validated learner scoring, or general reliability across every disorder/person.
