PSYCHLAB — DISORDER SELECTION RESEARCH
Research date: 8 October 2026
Scope: research only; no changes to the running app, agents, routing or API settings.

READ THIS FIRST
The main report is 01-Research-report.html. Open it in a browser.
It contains a grouped selection index, research findings and 68 candidate cards:
65 DSM-5-TR candidates, two separately labeled ICD-11 comparisons, and one
non-disorder/uncertainty comparator. This is a curated list, not every diagnosis,
subtype, specifier, induced condition or code in the DSM.

02-Disorder-catalogue.json contains the same research catalogue as structured data.
03-Age-history-and-future-design.txt explains age, timelines and later integration.
04-Source-register.json lists 56 references, their URLs, access level and use.
05-Search-method-and-gaps.txt documents search scope, access limits and open issues.
06-Selection-list.txt provides a quick plain-text grouped list.

None of these files is imported by PsychLab. No diagnostic selection screen was
added, and no Featherless requests were made for this research.

Evidence basis: public APA DSM-5/DSM-5-TR resources and updates; NIMH, WHO, CDC,
NHLBI, NIAAA, NIDCD, VA, AACAP and NICE resources; two secondary MSD clinical
reference pages. Source IDs resolve in the report and source register.

The publisher's full DSM-5-TR text returned an access error. This research does
not claim a full-manual review, reproduce diagnostic checklists, or establish
validated disorder simulation. Public summaries leave some exact criteria,
exclusions and age gates unresolved; those gaps are explicitly marked.

The proposed stages and authoring tiers are design recommendations, not limits
on which people can experience a condition. No numerical ages were invented.
