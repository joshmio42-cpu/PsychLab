PsychLab: natural conversation implementation notes
8 October 2026 (Asia/Manila)

What changed

The exact-statement selector was replaced with grounded conversational prose.
CORTEX routes the current question, LANGUAGE paraphrases only its permitted facts,
and AUDITOR checks the actual reply for support, relevance and hidden information.
The source of truth remains the frozen fictional case. Questions, plausible
assumptions, personality style and earlier generated replies are not evidence.

Brief greetings and acknowledgments have their own social route, with no case
facts and no automatic symptom summary. Substantive follow-ups retain their topic;
a newly named topic takes priority. Hobby questions can retrieve P02, P05 and P06,
while questions about symptoms or current enjoyment use the clinical case facts.

All eight profiles use ordinary spoken phrasing. Each has two newly authored,
stable casual facts giving concrete hobby details and an origin or motivation.
These are original fiction, not copied public patient histories or traits inferred
from a diagnosis. Caregiver profiles describe observations and the caregiver's
own reasons, not the child's unspoken motives. The separate profile-language note
lists every addition. Usual interests do not establish present enjoyment, ability,
frequency, symptom relief or recovery.

The missing-detail route uses one routing call and an explicit system message:
"That detail isn’t available for this interview." It does not invent a personal
denial, forgotten memory or privacy motive. Social and unavailable turns can be
saved in History but are excluded from clinical evidence selections. Existing
archives without interaction metadata remain readable. The transcript uses the
speaker's name; the page still identifies the interview as AI simulation.

Factual controls

Case IDs and response shape are validated. Independent narrow checks catch specified
clinical additions, unsupported numbers or time/frequency claims, and leaked internal
wording. The semantic audit must identify unsupported text spans and approve support,
relevance and non-disclosure. No rejected prose is displayed or silently replaced by
an authored answer. These checks reduce particular errors; a fallible model audit
does not prove universal factual correctness or clinical validity.

Budget and operation

Preparation and the local profile/disclosure rules make no model calls. A substantive
answer or generated social reply uses at most three calls. Missing-detail and other
system boundaries use one routing call. There are no automatic paid retries.
Successful duplicate request IDs reuse the saved result.

Output allowances per call: CORTEX 128, LANGUAGE 320, AUDITOR 160 tokens, totaling
608 for a full three-call turn. The audit allowance increased from 96 to 160;
the other role allowances did not change. The input cap remains 12000 UTF-8 bytes
per call, the default interview cap 60 call attempts and the server-run cap 300.
These are local usage controls, not account billing caps. Input tokens and failed
attempts also matter. Unreported provider usage remains unknown rather than zero.

The selected setup default is now Qwen/Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, following comparison
of focused live samples. An existing saved configuration or complete explicit
environment configuration determines the model at launch. The choice is reversible:
rerun Setup PsychLab.cmd, enter the same key privately, choose another exact model
ID and restart. Model choice does not require a new API key.

The final 72B sample used 46 calls across 16 turns, including one missing-detail
boundary requiring just one routing call. Provider usage totaled 34947 input tokens
and 1071 output tokens. At published rates of US$0.37/US$0.40 per million input/output
tokens, the estimated sample cost is US$0.01335879, approximately 1.34 US cents:
  (34947 x 0.37 + 1071 x 0.40) / 1000000
This is a sample estimate, not a verified bill, account balance or future guarantee.
Prices, context length, failures and model choice can change costs. Rate source:
https://featherless.ai/models/Qwen/Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct

Research-to-design connection

The links below support the design rationale; they do not validate this application.
Full evidence, source limitations and all eight sources are in 01-Research-notes.txt
and sources.json. Changes from human conversation research to this AI implementation
are engineering interpretations.

- Respond to the local social action instead of turning every exchange into an
  intake summary: Hudak and Maynard's analysis of small talk in consultations.
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9566.2011.01343.x

- Use ordinary daily activities and question-specific disclosure while preserving
  the case: Hooper et al.'s depression vignette development study.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2867621/

- Keep consistent role portrayal, respectful cases and a plan for unexpected
  questions: ASPE Standards of Best Practice, adapted from human participants.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41077-017-0043-4

- Resolve a particular unclear reference instead of repeating a whole script:
  Dingemanse et al.'s cross-language study of conversational repair.
  https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0136100

- Evaluate naturalness and case accuracy separately; neither fluent wording nor
  a successful small trial proves general realism: Erby et al.'s actor-performance
  study and Li et al.'s 2026 psychiatric virtual-patient study.
  https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/examination-of-standardized-patient-performance-accuracy-and-cons-4/
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12888-026-07896-3

Validation status

The final automated suite passed 130 checks. The final 72B live sample completed
16 turns without request errors, covering Jordan, Alex, Mika and Riley. This is an
operational result, not a claim that every accepted statement was factually validated.
For example, "interesting shapes" and an album "telling a story" are minor details
not established by the cited facts, yet passed the audit. The semantic checker is
fallible. The change improves conversational flexibility; it does not establish
all-cases reliability, diagnostic accuracy or educational effectiveness.

03-Live-evaluation.json records the final selected-model sample. Earlier replies,
failures and provider-format diagnostics remain in separately named JSON/JSONL
files, including the 30B and 32B attempts. They were not removed to present only
successful results. Read each file's actual model and outcomes; the presence of a
file is not a pass, and zero request errors are not the same as perfect grounding.
