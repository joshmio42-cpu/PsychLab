# Sources, attribution and third-party material

PsychLab separates original software and fictional examples from the evidence used to inform their design. Each research registry records its own sources and limitations; keep those records when sharing the project.

## Psychological and healthcare references

| Resource | How it is used here | Attribution and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NIMH, NICE, WHO and other public healthcare guidance | Original summaries, source links and case-design context | Guidance is not a license for clinical practice, an endorsement, or validation of the simulator |
| American Psychological Association materials | Links and assessment/education context | The local rubric is not an APA scoring standard |
| American Psychiatric Association / DSM-5-TR | Classification references and original educational descriptions | Full DSM text and proprietary criteria are not bundled; do not claim DSM certification or compliance |
| GAD-7 | Standard English items and fixed fictional responses in the legacy authored Alex case | Attribution and permission-source records S5–S7/S11 are retained in the foundation registry; simulated values are not real screening results |
| Research papers on personality, memory and simulated patients | Bibliographic references and original synthesis | Their evidence does not calibrate the project's authored coefficients or prove a learner benefit |

The [foundation source register](psych-simulator-foundation/data/sources.json) contains the GAD-7 attribution, the original validation-paper DOI, the Pfizer public-access announcement, the instrument instructions and the NHS-hosted wording reference used during development. Its recorded permission basis concerns the instrument, not unrelated host-site content, branding, translations or other tests.

Additional registries are in [personality evidence](psych-simulator-foundation/personality_system/evidence/sources.json), [disorder sources](psych-simulator-foundation/live/data/disorder-sources.json), and the dated [research collections](research/). These include author names and institutional names intentionally as scholarly attribution, not personal account information.

No full licensed DSM manual, secure commercial test bank or real patient record is included. Historical screenshots and handbook pages depict project-authored fictional cases. Referenced publications keep their own rights and terms.

## Software, models and services

The live JavaScript application uses Node.js built-ins and plain browser modules. A Node executable is not bundled; the optional Windows installer downloads a pinned official release with checksum and publisher verification. Node.js retains its own license.

Featherless is the live model-hosting integration. Adaption is an optional offline example-improvement workflow; it is not called on every interview turn, and the staged release is inactive. Each selected model and service has separate terms, licenses and account requirements. Provider credits and credentials are not included or transferred.

AI coding assistance was used in the implementation, research organization, tests and documentation. Historical source and experiment records are preserved for inspection. The project does not claim that AI-generated prose, code or synthetic case design has received clinician approval.
